System Requirement Specification & Feature Map: Netspeak Portal
1. Account Management & Authentication
• Deduplication Check:
o Validation rules prevent duplicate account creation based on exact matches on
Complete Name + Birthday or Cellphone Number.
• Profile Fields:
o Personal: Teacher Name (Display/Teaching Name), Real Complete Name,
Birthday, Cellphone Number, Emergency Contact Details, Complete Address.
o Academic: School Attended, Course and Major.
o Operational: Launch Date (Start Date), Project Type (FTEX, FT, TTP), Department
(Domestic, Overseas/Global), Assigned Rest Day, 51Talk Portal Login (Username,
Password).
• 2-Tier Approval Workflow:
o Step 1: Teacher submits registration form.
o Step 2: Admin Review verifies completeness and input accuracy.
o Step 3: Manager Final Approval confirms access.
o Step 4: Automated email delivery sends generated credentials upon final
approval.
2. Time, Attendance & Kiosk Management
• Freshness Check (Photo Capture):
o Camera selfie verification required at login; serves as official arrival timestamp.
• Shift Window & Alert Triggers:
o T-30 min: Early login window opens.
o T-10 min: Alert sent to Center Admin if the teacher has not completed the
Freshness Check.
o T-0 min (Shift Start): Real-time notification sent to Admin and Manager flagging
teacher as Late or Absent for immediate contact and outreach.
• Kiosk Portal Lockdown:

o Portal window locks during active shift hours.
o Logout button disabled until 15 minutes before shift end (or exact shift end).
o System prompts automated Windows/PC shutdown sequence upon successful
end-of-shift logout.
• Early Arrival / Late Stay Policy Modal:
o If logged in >2 hours prior to shift or remaining post-shift, an unclosable
banner/modal displays: "This workstation is strictly for official Netspeak work
purposes. Personal browsing is prohibited."
3. Daily Output Reporting & Workflows

• Grace Period & Reporting Edits:
o If unsubmitted, reminder alert prompts the teacher on the next day's login.
o Daily output reports allow edits/corrections for up to 48 hours post-shift.
4. Self-Service Requests & Approvals
• Switch Rest Day:
o Form collects Target Date, Swapped Date, and Reason.
o Automation Rule: Upon Manager approval, the weekly master schedule updates
rest days automatically for accurate shift tracking.
• Cash Loan:

• Equipment Replacement & Maintenance:
o Ticketing tab for hardware, headset, or PC peripheral issues.
• Early Time-Off (Auto-Approval Rule):
o Teacher submits request $\rightarrow$ Manager has 30 minutes to act.
o If manager takes no action within 30 minutes, request is auto-approved. System
displays a pop-up checklist reminding the teacher of steps to avoid client
tardiness.
• Bidirectional Notifications:
o All request status updates (Approved, Rejected, Pending Info) must push an
automated email/system notification back to the teacher with the reviewer's
notes.

Resigned, Terminated, Maternity Leave, Medical Leave).
o Inactivity Flag: Automatic alert sent to Admin and Manager if an Active teacher
